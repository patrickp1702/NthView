Glossary

Audit ch. 3
A planned check that the rules are being followed, using records and observation, by someone not doing the work.
Compliance ch. 2
Doing what the rules that apply to your work require, and being able to show that you did.
Compliance culture ch. 4
What people do when nobody is checking; the habits that make rules work or fail.
Policy ch. 3
A rule an organisation writes for itself, often to meet a law or standard in its own way.
Record ch. 2
Written evidence that a required check or action happened, when, and by whom.
Regulation ch. 3
A rule written by a government agency under a law, with the force of law behind it.
Speaking up ch. 4
Reporting a concern about a rule being broken through a channel the organisation protects.
