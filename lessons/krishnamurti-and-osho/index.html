Glossary

Choiceless awareness ch. 2
Krishnamurti's term for watching what is happening, inside and out, without judging, choosing or trying to change it.
Conditioning ch. 2
The habits of thought and feeling a mind picks up from family, culture, religion and experience, which both teachers say stand between us and seeing clearly.
Dynamic Meditation ch. 4
Osho's hour-long technique that moves from chaotic breathing and catharsis to stillness and celebration.
Order of the Star ch. 3
The organisation built around Krishnamurti as the coming World Teacher, which he dissolved in 1929.
Psychological time ch. 2
The sense of becoming something later; Krishnamurti held that it is the root of fear and conflict.
Witnessing ch. 4
Osho's word for observing thoughts and emotions without becoming them; close to, but reached differently from, choiceless awareness.
Zorba the Buddha ch. 4
Osho's image of a complete human being: worldly joy and inner silence in one person.
